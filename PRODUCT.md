## Product Purpose
A single, shareable essay-site that (1) states Leul's personal definition of AGI in his own words, (2) separates AGI from godlike ASI ("it won't cure cancer or solve world hunger in a snap, and it doesn't need to"), and (3) honestly assesses how closely reality (as of late September 2026) is tracking the AI 2027 scenario at ai-2027.com, explained in direct, plain, human language. Success: a reader finishes knowing Leul's definition, what AI 2027 predicts, and a fair verdict on where it is right, early, or late.

## Positioning
Not a hype page and not a doom page. A personal, checkable definition ("one agent, professional level, almost any field, no constant human guidance") used as the yardstick to grade a famous forecast against dated evidence.

## Operating Context
